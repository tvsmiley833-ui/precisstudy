It's free

StudyStacks doesn't charge for anything, doesn't have a paid tier, and doesn't collect payment information. If that ever changes, it'll be clearly announced — nothing will silently start costing money.

Use it for studying, not for anything harmful

Please don't treat anything here as a substitute for your teacher, textbook, or official exam prep materials — always double-check anything you're unsure about.
