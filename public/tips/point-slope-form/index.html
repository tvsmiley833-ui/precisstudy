Algebra 1

Point-Slope Form: Write a Line From a Point and Slope

How to use point-slope form to write a line, with two worked examples.

The formula

  • y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)
  • m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁)

Use it when you know a point (x₁, y₁) and the slope m.

Example 1

Write the line through (2, 5) with slope 3.

  1. y − 5 = 3(x − 2)
  2. y − 5 = 3x − 6
y = 3x − 1

Example 2

Write the line through (1, 2) and (3, 8).

  1. m = (8 − 2)/(3 − 1) = 3
  2. y − 2 = 3(x − 1)
y = 3x − 1

Common mistake

y − 5 = 3(x + 2) for the point (2, 5)

The signs flip: a point (2, 5) gives (x − 2) and (y − 5).

A point like (−2, 5) gives (x + 2).
