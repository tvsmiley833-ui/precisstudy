Unit 7: Grammar & Mechanics Essentials

Unit 1: Elements of Story

Watch out
Climax ≠ ending — resolutions come AFTER the climax
Watch out
Antagonist isn't automatically evil; it's whatever blocks the protagonist
Watch out
First-person narrators can lie or misread — don't equate narrator with author

Unit 2: Figurative Language & Literary Devices

Watch out
'The rain cried' is personification, NOT pathetic fallacy confusion — keep terms precise
Watch out
Hyperbole isn't meant literally — flagging it as a 'lie' misses the point
Watch out
Verbal irony ≠ coincidence: situational irony needs expectation violated

Unit 3: Symbolism, Theme & Motif

Watch out
'Love' is a TOPIC not a theme — theme must assert something about love
Watch out
Don't hunt symbols first and force theme later; patterns emerge FROM reading
Watch out
Motif ≠ symbol strictly: motif recurs; a symbol can fire once

Unit 4: Poetry Essentials

Watch out
Free verse isn't formless — line breaks and stanzas still make meaning
Watch out
Rhyme SCHEME letters track END sounds only, not internal rhymes
Watch out
The volta is a TURN in thought, not just another line

Unit 5: The Epic & The Odyssey

Watch out
The Odyssey doesn't begin with Odysseus — it opens on Telemachus (the Telemachy)
Watch out
Hubris means overreaching pride against gods' order, not just 'confidence'
Watch out
Aeolus's wind bag fails because the CREW opens it — distrust, not Odysseus's sleep alone

Unit 6: Rhetoric & Persuasion

Watch out
A counterargument isn't weakness — omitting it is
Watch out
Pathos isn't automatically bad; unearned pathos is the problem
Watch out
Correlation in 'evidence' isn't causation — watch for leap-in-logic logos

Unit 7: Grammar & Mechanics Essentials

Watch out
'Between you and me' — prepositions take OBJECT case; 'between you and I' is the hypercorrection error
Watch out
Nonessential appositive commas change meaning: my sister Ann (essential) vs my sister, Ann (one sister only)
Watch out
Fewer for countables, less for amounts: fewer calories, less sugar

Unit 8: Writing: Thesis, Evidence & Paragraph Craft

Watch out
Plot summary ≠ analysis — every body paragraph must ARGUE something
Watch out
'In conclusion…' announcements weaken endings; just conclude
Watch out
Announcing a topic ('I will write about courage') isn't a thesis
