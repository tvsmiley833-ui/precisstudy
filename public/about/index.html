Who's behind PrecisStudy

Built by a student,
for students.

Hi — I'm a high schooler, and I built PrecisStudy.

PrecisStudy is a free study resource with quizzes, flashcards, practice tests, and an AI-powered study assistant to help you work through them.

I built PrecisStudy because I wanted a simple, easy to access and use database of study guides for everything — rather than searching through PDFs or paying for apps with guides I can't customize or edit.

One more thing worth being upfront about: I'm one student, not a publishing company, and I build the study guides and practice questions with a lot of help from AI. Everything is built around standard curriculum concepts, and I go back through units myself, but with hundreds of questions across multiple subjects, mistakes slip through sometimes — I've found and fixed a few myself. If you ever hit a question that seems wrong, a broken explanation, or anything that just doesn't add up, please tell me. I'd much rather fix it than have you trust something that's off.

PrecisStudy is completely free, and it's going to stay that way. If you have any requests for PrecisStudy, or want to tell me about a class you think we need a study guide for, feel free to contact me here, or at .
