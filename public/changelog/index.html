Changelog

Everything that's shipped, newest first — plain English, no marketing spin. See something you asked for? It's probably down there.

September 14, 2026
  • NewAdaptive diagnostic routing — the diagnostic now sends you extra questions on whichever units you're still weak in, instead of stopping after one fixed round.
  • NewFuzzy-match flashcard typing — a "Type it" mode that accepts close-enough spelling, not just an exact match.
  • NewAI concept breakdown — highlight any confusing sentence in a guide and ask the AI helper to explain it on the spot.
  • NewInvite links — get a personal link from Settings to invite classmates; free for them too.
  • NewAn open-access banner on every page, so it's obvious up front: no paywalls, no sign-up wall.
  • NewSubscribe to your weekly study schedule as a calendar feed (Apple/Google/Outlook).
  • NewAn in-app feedback button on every page, with an admin queue to triage what comes in.
  • NewUpload a PDF, Word doc, or photo of your notes and generate a flashcard deck from it.
  • NewSmart hints, a session-summary screen after each quiz, distraction-free reading mode, and a dashboard greeting.
  • PerfShared scripts are now content-hashed and cached for a year in the browser — pages load faster on repeat visits.
September 12, 2026
  • NewGoogle Classroom + Calendar connect, so assignments and due dates show up right on your dashboard.
  • NewA full-length, original SAT practice exam with real section timing.
  • NewPer-unit progress bars, a keyboard shortcuts overlay, and small interaction polish across the guide.
  • NewHomepage search autocomplete, subject category pills, and a "/" shortcut to jump straight to search.
  • NewA quick-reference drawer and an optional Desmos calculator, available from every guide.
  • NewPublished our Parents' Bill of Rights for Data Privacy and Security.
  • NewA mistake log plus difficulty and topic tags on every quiz question.

  • PerfSelf-hosted every guide's fonts and dropped the render-blocking Google Fonts request site-wide.
