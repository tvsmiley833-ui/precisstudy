    Build your weekly schedule

    Tell us when you're free and what you want to study — we'll build a color-coded weekly plan. Click any block to jump straight into that study guide.

    ❔ How this works (3 steps)
    1. Paint your free time. Click or drag across the grid below for each day you can study. Click a filled cell again to clear just that hour.
    2. Pick your subjects. Check off the classes you want scheduled, grouped by type below. If you check more than one, use the ↑↓ arrows to rank them — higher priority gets more of the free days.
    3. Build it. Click "Build my schedule" — each colored block links straight to that subject's next study unit.
